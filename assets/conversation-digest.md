## Revise the evolving model

Follow [person-model](../references/person-model.md). Evaluate relevant values/priorities, decision criteria, communication patterns, supported motivations, tensions and competing hypotheses against actual supplied evidence. A digest must revise or deliberately retain the model, not only summarize the meeting.

| Prior contextual hypothesis | Actual supporting / contrary evidence | Alternative explanation / changed context | Revised hypothesis and qualitative confidence | Remaining gap / disconfirmation / next learning aim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | |

Strengthen, narrow, weaken, retire or leave unresolved with a reason. Do not manufacture change when evidence is absent. Hypotheses are not facts. Preserve source history, user edits and identity anchors. Agreement or a successful outcome does not validate an interpretation.

## Verified update and next preparation

**Intended memory delta and source basis:** Add / modify / correct / supersede / deduplicate / reversible identity repair / authorized remove.
**User edits, source history and dependent views preserved or affected:**
**Applied status:** Proposed / attempted / confirmed / uncertain, separately where needed.
**Inspection and retrieval evidence:** Verify current understanding and guidance, preserved corrections and absence of duplicate authoritative records. Inspect uncertain writes before retrying.
**Remaining gaps or stale projections:**
**Next-PREPARE implications:** What the revised model suggests the human should remember, reconsider, follow through on or leave alone, with reasons and alternatives. Include a small optional information-gathering move tied to a remaining uncertainty, its private learning aim, plausible interpretations, disconfirmation/limits and natural optional follow-up branches.

Read-only or unavailable persistence produces an unapplied proposal. Write-only access cannot establish recalled or deduplicated current memory. Prepare shared output for its requested audience and purpose.
