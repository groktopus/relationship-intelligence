# Interests, disagreement, genuine value and information boundaries

**Method baseline 1.1 · provisional. Evidence baseline: 2026-10-23, approved specification Revision 6.** These are evidence-informed behavioral contracts, not validated interventions or assessment instruments. IDs remain stable across revisions; preserve the version and evidence baseline with any rubric or evaluation using them. A source revision requires explicit review, not a silent change to a frozen evaluation.

## Purpose: an evolving understanding of the person and relationship

PREPARE and DIGEST serve a deeper, revisable evidence-grounded model of this person in this relationship: values and priorities, decision criteria, communication patterns, supported motivations and tensions, competing explanations, and consequential gaps. Use references/person-model.md for the model structure and safeguards ([person model](person-model.md)). “Digital twin” is an aspiration to useful contextual understanding, not literal replication or validated psychology. 

## Asynchronous use: PREPARE and DIGEST

**Product orientation 0.2.0: prepare the human before a conversation; digest notes or, preferably, a transcript afterward.** Adaptation happens across conversations. The agent does not monitor, manage or steer the live exchange. Conversational moves below are optional approaches for the human to consider, never instructions the agent executes on the other person.

In **PREPARE**, recall the authorized person/relationship model and identify a consequential uncertainty between competing hypotheses. Offer a small selection of personalized investigative approaches with reasons and a private account of what their possible answers could teach. A possible question or reflection must fit the history, vocabulary and purpose; avoid a script, required sequence or quota. The human chooses whether and how to use it, including taking practical action or making no inquiry.

In **DIGEST**, inspect what the supplied transcript or notes actually establish. Preparation suggestions are not evidence that a question was asked, an answer occurred, or agreement formed. Prefer a transcript for wording and sequence; notes may be selective recollections. Attribute uncertain speakers tentatively, preserve missing context and distinguish quoted words from paraphrase. Do not invent responses, motives, pauses or nonverbal behavior. Check interpretations against corrections and contrary evidence, then enrich, narrow or overturn the person/relationship hypotheses, revise the next preparation and propose any authorized memory change. A summary alone is not this model update. There is no presumed live feedback channel.

Load for an actual decision involving priorities, tradeoffs, a benefit hypothesis or disclosure risks. A sales account alone is no reason to activate negotiation. New curiosity and value can develop through an honest conversation; preexisting purchase interest is not required. Natural curiosity and legitimate professional persuasion can deepen understanding and develop that opportunity together. Investigate what would make the option valuable to this person, then let their actual criteria and feedback shape the proposal.

## From a proposal to a testable benefit

Start from the shared decision and voluntarily expressed goals. Separate a stated position from a possible underlying concern without assuming you know that concern. Compare feasible options and what each would need to accomplish. Hold reasons for and against action together. Disagreement may concern facts, predictions, priorities, authority, costs or a firm boundary; those require different responses.

A value proposition is a hypothesis: identify the benefit, who experiences it, the supporting evidence, key assumptions, total cost, risk and relevant alternatives. Quantified savings need their own substantiation. Interest expressed during discussion is neither a verified benefit nor a commitment. Offer an informative comparison or bounded next step when appropriate; recommend no action if the case fails. Understanding the counterpart's position does not require agreement with it.

## Interpret information under incentives

Distinguish a statement’s content, its source and the incentives surrounding it. Some details are unavailable, selectively disclosed or outside the speaker’s knowledge. A useful preparation identifies which uncertainties matter to the decision and which can be checked at a relevant level of detail. DIGEST retains interested claims as claims until consequential facts can be corroborated. Inconsistency can reflect changed context, incomplete information or misunderstanding; compare those explanations before drawing a conclusion about accuracy.

## Learn from choices and tradeoffs

Actual choices and voluntary explanations may distinguish competing accounts of what this person values, which criteria govern a decision, and which tensions matter in this relationship. PREPARE should identify what an optional comparison could teach and what would weaken the favored account. DIGEST should update those hypotheses from the actual record, not convert acceptance into proof of a hidden motivation. A rejected apparently beneficial option may reveal an overlooked cost, changed context or another criterion; it is not evidence of irrationality. An unanswered comparison remains a model gap rather than evidence for a preferred explanation.

## MC-07: Explore interests, priorities and tradeoffs

**Method baseline 1.1 · provisional; evidence baseline 2026-10-23.**

- **Uncertainty addressed:** Whether an expressed position reflects a particular constraint or priority, and whether legitimate tradeoffs exist.
- **Context:** A real collaborative decision or negotiation with permission to discuss relevant issues.
- **Evidence needed:** Stated positions, voluntary accounts of priorities, feasible options and material constraints. Never assume unspoken interests are known.
- **Possible moves:** PREPARE: suggest an optional discussion of what an option must accomplish, a non-sensitive priority comparison or conditional option, with its decision relevance. DIGEST: record actual priorities, constraints, differences and accepted or rejected tradeoffs without assuming the planned discussion occurred.
- **Alternatives:** Interests may be genuinely incompatible; a position may be a firm boundary. Public claims may be strategic, mistaken or incomplete without being lies.
- **Disconfirmation:** The actual record shows a tradeoff violates a constraint, parties value it differently, or disclosure risk outweighs usefulness; revise the next recommendation rather than assume common ground.
- **Stopping:** No feasible acceptable option, missing authority or refusal ends the recommended exploration. No agreement is a valid digest and preparation outcome.
- **Source depth:** [NEG-04](evidence-index.md#neg-04) full practitioner framework; [NEG-03](evidence-index.md#neg-03) full disclosure-behavior experiment; [NEG-01](evidence-index.md#neg-01) original abstract supports background only. No exact technique or empirical safety claim prescribed from the abstract.

## MC-08: Understand disagreement and develop genuine value

**Method baseline 1.1 · provisional; evidence baseline 2026-10-23.**

- **Uncertainty addressed:** Whether disagreement reflects different priorities, beliefs, predictions, constraints or actual incompatibility.
- **Context:** Product exploration, collaboration or negotiation where new interest could be useful but is not presumed.
- **Evidence needed:** The person’s relevant goals, option evidence, costs, risks, assumptions and alternatives. Separate facts from predictions.
- **Possible moves:** PREPARE: frame a benefit as a testable hypothesis, compare options and assumptions, or recommend no action. DIGEST: inspect actual concerns and option evidence, distinguish facts from predictions, and update the value case or record incompatibility.
- **Alternatives:** Hesitation may be a rational response to weak evidence, price, timing or no fit—not defensiveness or a flaw.
- **Disconfirmation:** Benefit evidence fails, a competing option is better, priorities change or the person rejects the framing in the supplied record. Revise the value case and next preparation.
- **Stopping:** No fit, no sale, a firm boundary or a preferable alternative is a valid end. Do not recommend repeated persuasion to overcome it.
- **Source depth:** [SALE-03](evidence-index.md#sale-03) full qualitative conceptualization and [NEG-04](evidence-index.md#neg-04) full professional framework. Neither demonstrates efficacy of this contract; claims of material benefits require their own evidence.

## MC-09: Interpret information under strategic incentives

**Method baseline 1.1 · provisional; evidence baseline 2026-10-23.**

- **Uncertainty addressed:** Which claims can be relied on and which information is appropriate to request, retain or disclose.
- **Context:** Negotiation, competing incentives, third-party information or commercially sensitive material.
- **Evidence needed:** Source/provenance, known incentives, relevance, user authority and disclosure constraints. Confidence in rapport is insufficient.
- **Possible moves:** PREPARE: identify which information would distinguish the live hypotheses and which checks could corroborate consequential claims. DIGEST: distinguish actual claims, independent corroboration and unresolved information; preserve incentives and disclosure context.
- **Alternatives:** Omission can protect privacy or confidentiality; inconsistent accounts can reflect uncertainty or misunderstanding. Neither proves deception.
- **Disconfirmation:** Legitimate independent evidence contradicts a claim; retain the contradiction and consider an optional clarification for a later conversation without accusation or diagnosis.
- **Stopping:** Protected information, no authority, irrelevance or refusal stops recommended inquiry or disclosure. Do not request a bottom line, outside option or third-party secret merely to enrich a model.
- **Source depth:** [NEG-03](evidence-index.md#neg-03) full experiment demonstrates disclosure effects, not safety; [NEG-02](evidence-index.md#neg-02) publisher abstract is a caution only, not a lie detector or tactic; [NEG-05](evidence-index.md#neg-05) full practitioner risk guidance. Disclosure behavior is not a measure of information quality or mutual benefit.

## Accepting incompatibility

If no feasible option respects material constraints, state the mismatch plainly. No agreement, no sale, a preferable alternative or a pause can be successful outputs of good reasoning. Do not keep eliciting objections until the person yields or treat a changed mind as proof of agent efficacy. Useful learning, mutual fit and incremental sales are separate outcomes.

NEG-03 shows that requests and sharing change disclosure behavior, including risky categories; it does not show that more disclosure is beneficial. NEG-01/02 are abstract-only background/caution and license no exact protocol. NEG-04/05 are practitioner frameworks. SALE-03 supplies a qualitative model, not a causal performance result. Source links, nulls and depth: references/evidence-index.md.
