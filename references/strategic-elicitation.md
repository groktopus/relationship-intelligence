# Strategic elicitation and informative correction

Use when preparing an optional conversational approach to distinguish consequential person-model hypotheses, or digesting an exchange where a statement prompted elaboration or correction. The purpose is progressively deeper understanding of values, motivations and decision criteria through natural conversation. PREPARE equips the human; DIGEST interprets the actual record afterward. See [person model](person-model.md) for the underlying hypothesis structure.

## Separate conversational form from information quality

A question is one way to invite an account. A tentative summary, grounded observation or comparison of actual experiences can also invite elaboration. Choose the form that makes the unresolved distinction easiest to discuss. Its value is whether the resulting evidence changes the model, not how much the person says or whether the move resembles an intelligence technique.

Consider what the contribution introduced. A broad statement can frame the person's answer; a specific assertion can supply information the person later repeats. Agreement, correction, silence and elaboration have different evidential meanings. A correction establishes the speaker's account of the disputed point, not automatic truth of every accompanying detail or a general psychological trait.

## What the named techniques mean, and what is supported

| Form | Description in the retained sources | Evidence and transfer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural conversation | Professional doctrine describes purposeful information gathering that resembles ordinary social or professional exchange. | Doctrine supplies descriptions, not an experimentally established advantage for person understanding. Naturalness alone does not establish accurate answers. |
| Generalized statement | A broad proposition invites someone to locate their experience relative to it. This label is not a separately standardized intervention in the studies recovered here. | A practitioner's account of presumptive statements offers anecdotes, not a causal comparison of generalized statements with genuine questions. Treat a generalization as a proposed frame that may need correction, not a fact about this person. |
| Deliberately false statement | Doctrine and practitioner accounts describe intentionally inaccurate assertions intended to prompt correction. | The reviewed sources do not establish a universal corrective impulse or validate this isolated technique for relationship-model accuracy. A HUMINT study comparing confirmation/disconfirmation variants does not by itself validate every false-statement recipe. |
| Correction invitation | A tentative understanding gives the speaker a concrete interpretation to refine or reject. | This skill uses the methodological principle of revisable understanding. Its application to this agent is a design choice; a correction should change the hypothesis and future preparation. |
| Artificial or feigned ignorance | Professional descriptions present an apparent knowledge gap as an invitation for the other person to explain. | Official-source search excerpts and a copied FBI brochure document the description. An independently tested benefit for ordinary relationship understanding was not established in this targeted search. Genuine uncertainty can directly motivate an explanation request without pretending the prior evidence is absent. |

These distinctions matter methodologically. A grounded tentative interpretation and a knowingly false assertion can both receive a correction, but they create different starting conditions. Preserve the original premise in DIGEST: information introduced by the human is not independent corroboration when it returns in the answer. When a broad claim narrows the answer space, consider whether an open invitation would discriminate the hypotheses more cleanly.

## Design a useful PREPARE move

Begin with a contrast in actual shared history. For instance, someone welcomed a small experiment but declined a wider rollout. Competing explanations might concern reversibility, capacity, the quality of evidence or the consequences for another team. A useful approach revisits what differed between those real decisions. A grounded tentative reflection may help where the person has already explained part of the contrast; an open question fits where the explanation is largely unknown.

Privately give the human the learning aim: which distinction matters, why it changes collaboration or a proposal, and what the evidence already suggests. Anticipate several plausible interpretations. A workload explanation could favor a smaller scope or different timing; an evidence requirement could favor a staged demonstration; rejection of the benefit could require a different proposition or no further action. An answer can support several explanations together.

Identify counterevidence before favoring an account. If a low-effort reversible option is also declined, that weakens a workload-only explanation without proving a hidden motive. A natural follow-up clarifies the distinction actually raised, rather than executing a planned sequence. If the practical meaning is sufficient or the topic is declined, leave the remaining model gap unresolved. Use the person's language and this relationship's history; do not copy the illustration as a routine probe.

## DIGEST the learning rather than the tactic

Record the actual initiating question or statement separately from the person's response. Attribute exact transcript wording, paraphrased notes and uncertain speakers accordingly. A planned correction invitation supplies no evidence if it was never used.

Compare the response with each active explanation. State what gained support, weakened, narrowed or was overturned, with a qualitative reason tied to the actual evidence. If a correction changes the meaning of a metaphor or the relative importance of a criterion, replace the old interpretation and recompute future guidance. A person explaining expertise in response to apparent ignorance does not establish vanity; correcting an error does not establish a need for superiority. Those interpretations require their own discriminating evidence.

A richer model can emerge from precise contextual distinctions: the same person may want autonomy in routine work and coordination when another team's commitments depend on them. Track the condition that changes the preference. Repeated agreement with a human-supplied premise is weaker evidence of such a pattern than distinct, independently grounded examples.

## Evidence and access depth

All access below: 2026-10-23. This is a targeted review, not a systematic search or a local efficacy test.

- **SE-01, professional doctrine, official search excerpts only:** [DCSA Elicitation brochure (2021)](https://www.dcsa.mil/Portals/91/Documents/CI/DCSA-CI_Elicitation_2021.pdf) describes false assertions prompting correction. Full scrape failed and download returned 403; unseen content is not treated as inspected.
- **SE-02, professional job aid, official search excerpts only:** [CDSE, Accidental Conversations](https://www.cdse.edu/Portals/124/Documents/jobaids/ci/Accidental-Conversations.pdf) names feigned ignorance among knowledge-gap-oriented approaches. Full scrape/download failed. Its title's reference to science is not evidence that each named technique has an independent outcome test.
- **SE-03, attributed doctrine through a third-party copy:** [2015 copy of FBI elicitation material](https://blog.cyberwar.nl/2015/06/fbi-web-page-on-elicitation-techniques-copied-from-fbi-gov/) supplies full descriptions of false statements, feigned ignorance and ordinary-conversation framing. The original provenance is asserted by the copying page; current official text was not recovered. Descriptive doctrine, not independent validation.
- **SE-04, first-person practitioner illustration, full page:** [Schafer, The Art of Elicitation, Part 1 (2012)](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/let-their-words-do-the-talking/201204/the-art-of-elicitation-part-1) explains presumptive statements using anecdotes. It supplies no controlled comparison, sampling frame or test of the asserted corrective mechanism.
- **SE-05, original empirical study, publisher and institutional abstracts only:** [May, Granhag and Oleszkiewicz (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1002/jip.1412) compares two Scharff variants with a direct approach in 90 mock-source phone interviews. The abstract reports greater new-information yield for the Scharff approach, misestimation of disclosure, and better masking of objectives for confirmation than disconfirmation. This is a bundled approach in an instructed role-play, not evidence that every individual component improves person understanding; full methods/moderators were not inspected. The [institutional abstract](https://research.utwente.nl/en/publications/eliciting-intelligence-using-the-scharff-technique-closing-in-on-/) repeats the same study, not independent corroboration.
- **SE-06, original empirical component study, full publisher article:** [Using the Scharff-technique to elicit information: How to effectively establish the “illusion of knowing it all”?](https://www.elsevier.es/en-revista-the-european-journal-psychology-applied-381-articulo-using-scharff-technique-elicit-information-how-S1889186116300014) reports two randomized experiments, each with 60 mostly student participants. Effects on perceived interviewer knowledge and knowledge-gap searching were mixed: the first experiment found a global knowledge-perception difference but not the predicted search differences; the second found differences in listed gaps and gap searching but none in global gap perception. The expected linkage between searching and global perceptions was not found. Participants could not disclose case-specific information or ask questions during the interrupted phone exchanges. Consequently this component study does not test new-information yield, conversational accuracy or relationship-model improvement.

The operative transfer is narrower than the doctrine's collection goals: formulate informative alternatives, invite a genuine account, retain the prompting context, and let correction alter the model. Independent comparisons of generalized statements, intentional error and artificial ignorance on accurate, durable person understanding remain unresolved by the inspected evidence.
