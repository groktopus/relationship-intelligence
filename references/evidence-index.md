# Evidence index

**Evidence baseline 2026-10-23 · method baseline 1.1 · provisional.** This focused English-language review is selective, not systematic. The combined asynchronous preparation/digest method has no demonstrated efficacy, whole-person validity or model-path superiority. Full access permits closer scrutiny, not automatic generalization. Abstract-only entries support background/caution within the stated depth, never unseen methods or exact protocols.

## Purpose: an evolving understanding of the person and relationship

PREPARE and DIGEST serve a deeper, revisable evidence-grounded model of this person in this relationship: values and priorities, decision criteria, communication patterns, supported motivations and tensions, competing explanations, and consequential gaps. Use references/person-model.md for the model structure and safeguards ([person model](person-model.md)). “Digital twin” is an aspiration to useful contextual understanding, not literal replication or validated psychology. 

## Asynchronous use: PREPARE and DIGEST

**Product orientation 0.2.0: prepare the human before a conversation; digest notes or, preferably, a transcript afterward.** Adaptation happens across conversations. The agent does not monitor, manage or steer the live exchange. Conversational moves below are optional approaches for the human to consider, never instructions the agent executes on the other person.

In **PREPARE**, recall the authorized person/relationship model and identify a consequential uncertainty between competing hypotheses. Offer a small selection of personalized investigative approaches with reasons and a private account of what their possible answers could teach. A possible question or reflection must fit the history, vocabulary and purpose; avoid a script, required sequence or quota. The human chooses whether and how to use it, including taking practical action or making no inquiry.

In **DIGEST**, inspect what the supplied transcript or notes actually establish. Preparation suggestions are not evidence that a question was asked, an answer occurred, or agreement formed. Prefer a transcript for wording and sequence; notes may be selective recollections. Attribute uncertain speakers tentatively, preserve missing context and distinguish quoted words from paraphrase. Do not invent responses, motives, pauses or nonverbal behavior. Check interpretations against corrections and contrary evidence, then enrich, narrow or overturn the person/relationship hypotheses, revise the next preparation and propose any authorized memory change. A summary alone is not this model update. There is no presumed live feedback channel.

Empirical studies, correlational syntheses, methodological guidance, practitioner frameworks and correction notices answer different questions. Nonsignificance does not prove equivalence or universal failure. Repeated analyses of one dataset are not independent replication.

Method routing from the skill root: references/focus-and-elicitation.md (MC-01/02); references/listening-and-pacing.md (MC-03/04/05); references/context-and-revision.md (MC-06/11); references/negotiation-and-value.md (MC-07/08/09); references/calm-disagreement.md (MC-10). These source notes are optional support checks, not a mandatory reading chain. Stable IDs below are the citation keys.

## Evidence constrains the person model

The sources motivate careful participant meaning, contextual attribution, responsiveness and revision. They do not validate a deep psychological replica, fixed answer-to-trait mapping or confidence percentage. PREPARE uses competing, evidenced hypotheses to make optional inquiry informative; DIGEST tests them against actual notes/transcripts. Values, motivations, decision criteria, communication patterns and tensions require their own attributed, scoped support and counterevidence. See references/person-model.md for the model contract; source-supported conversational ingredients do not validate that composite architecture.

## INT-01

[Interviewing to develop Patient-Reported Outcome measures for clinical research: eliciting patients’ experience (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3933509/)

**Methodological guidance; full PMC article.** PRO-development interviewing guidance describes flexible guides, open probes, silence, reflection and quality checks. This clinical content-validity methodology is not an efficacy trial or validated sales/personality instrument. Use purpose and coverage to guide attention; interviewer bias, leading wording and premature interpretation remain risks.

## CL-01

[Tosey, Lawley & Meese (2014), Eliciting Metaphor through Clean Language](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-8551.12042)

**Qualitative methodological study; publisher abstract only.** Six managers illustrate participant-originated metaphor elicitation. Small purposive sample and practitioner involvement limit transfer. No independent comparison, diagnostic validity or superiority follows from the accessed abstract. Preserve the speaker’s meaning rather than decode symbols.

## CL-02

[Nehyba & Lawley (2020), Clean Language Interviewing as a Second-Person Method in the Science of Consciousness](https://cleanlanguage.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/NehybaLawley2020-Clean-Language-Interviewing-JoCS-PREPRINT.pdf)

**Methodological/rating analysis; full author preprint.** Nineteen interviews across five projects assess interviewer-introduced content; reliability varies by rater/project. Method adherence does not establish answer truth, recollection reliability, personality validity or sales efficacy. Authors explicitly caution against that inference. No independent Clean Language superiority is demonstrated; use only to inspect leading assumptions and participant vocabulary.

## ASK-01

[Huang et al. (2017), It Does Not Hurt to Ask: Question-Asking Increases Liking](https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/download.aspx?name=Huang%20et%20al%202017.pdf)

**Original experiments plus observational field study; full author manuscript.** Getting-acquainted experiments support liking/responsiveness effects of manipulated question frequency; speed-dating follow-up associations are contested. Liking, second-date offers and understanding accuracy are different outcomes. Read with ASK-02/03/04; no quota, universal listening metric or adaptive agent rubric follows.

## ASK-02

[Kluger & Malloy (2019), Question Asking as a Dyadic Behavior](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6857721/)

**Methodological critique/reanalysis; full PMC text.** Reanalyzes the original speed-dating data using actor/partner/dyad decomposition and distinguishes asking from listening. Some associations are absent or negative in this analysis. Dataset reuse is not independent replication; model/variable choices are contested in ASK-03. This index does not adjudicate the competing code.

## ASK-03

[Yeomans et al. (2019), It Helps to Ask: The Cumulative Benefits of Asking Follow-Up Questions](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/yeomans_huang_brooks_minson___gino_2019_.___6cf7b840-7ad0-4459-8931-81768f821854.pdf)

**Original authors’ methodological reply/reanalysis; full author manuscript.** Defends cumulative follow-up benefits and disputes variables/analytical choices in ASK-02. A model controlling actor/partner effects lacks an effect; authors contest its interpretation. This is the same evidence family, not independent confirmation. Preserve the dispute rather than select the convenient finding.

## ASK-04

[Correction to Huang et al. (2017), published2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40111841/)

**Publisher correction notice; full correction abstract.** The 2025 notice reports minor reporting corrections, unchanged substantive hypothesis-test conclusions and no identified data-collection-integrity concern after an audit. It is neither a retraction nor this skill’s independent audit/replication; it does not settle every modeling dispute. Avoid fragile original numerical prescriptions and retain ASK-02/03 alongside it.

## LISTEN-01

[Weger, Castle Bell, Minei & Robinson (2014), Relative Effectiveness of Active Listening in Initial Interactions](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10904018.2013.813234)

**Original randomized laboratory experiment; full publisher text.** Among 115 undergraduates in brief confederate interactions, active-listening responses improved reported understanding versus advice/acknowledgment. The intervention bundles paraphrase/elaboration and controlled nonverbal involvement. Satisfaction/attraction differences versus advice were nonsignificant, which does not establish equivalence. Explicit requests for advice can make advice appropriate.

## LISTEN-02

[West et al. (2025), High-quality listening behaviors linked to social connection between strangers](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44271-025-00342-2)

**Preregistered secondary analyses of two intervention datasets; full publisher article.** Observed listening relates to several connection outcomes, but behavior itself was not randomly assigned. Context and measures matter: Study 1 lacks a participant-reported connection association; Study 2 follow-ups link only to partner response time, not positivity resonance, and its intervention did not significantly change listening. Practiced partners/university samples and nonverbal measures limit transcript transfer.

## MI-01

[Magill et al. (2018), A Meta-Analysis of Motivational Interviewing Process](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1zz078bv)

**Correlational process meta-analysis; institutional abstract only.** Clinical behavior-change synthesis finds MI-consistent skills associated with both change and sustain talk. Proportion-based technical paths were supported, while independently tested change talk and the relational hypothesis were not. Denominators matter. Neither enthusiasm nor more favorable talk establishes readiness; clinical mechanisms do not establish effects for this professional preparation workflow.

## MI-02

[SAMHSA TIP35, Chapter3: Motivational Interviewing as a Counseling Style](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK571068/)

**Official clinical professional guidance; full chapter.** SAMHSA describes reflection as a hypothesis to check/revise, plus summaries and autonomy. This is counseling guidance in substance-use treatment, not empirical proof of these agent contracts. Borrow tentative understanding and respect for mixed views; do not import treatment claims or therapist-directed purchase “change talk.”

## CRISIS-01

[FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin: An Evaluation Tool for Crisis Negotiators](https://leb.fbi.gov/articles/focus/focus-on-training-an-evaluation-tool-for-crisis-negotiators)

**Official practitioner training framework; full article.** FBI training descriptions include paraphrase, summary, open questions, pause and tentative emotion labeling. This is not a controlled effectiveness study or a calibrated person score. Crisis conditions and police authority differ fundamentally from free ordinary discussion; control/compliance objectives and training targets do not transfer.

## CRISIS-02

[FBI Law Enforcement Bulletin: Leading Through Listening](https://leb.fbi.gov/articles/focus/focus-on-leadership-leading-through-listening)

**Official leadership practitioner guidance; full article.** Describes attention, withheld judgment, clarification, reflection, summary and space before problem solving. Practical guidance is not independent causal evidence. Emotion interpretations remain tentative; no lie detector, trait assessment or influence-success guarantee follows.

## ATTR-01

[Gawronski (2003), On Difficult Questions and Evident Answers](https://www.bertramgawronski.com/documents/G2003PSPB.pdf)

**Four original quiz-role experiments; full author manuscript.** Situational difficulty, role salience and processing conditions affect dispositional knowledge judgments. Some situational sensitivity persists under distraction; some interactions/mediation are marginal. Neither stable traits nor a universal failure to notice context follows. These constrained laboratory results do not establish accuracy about a particular collaborator.

## PER-01

[Human et al. (2013), Accurate First Impressions Leave a Lasting Impression](https://repository.essex.ac.uk/15319/1/Accurate%20First%20Impressions%20leave%20a%20lasting%20impression.pdf)

**Original longitudinal observational study; full institutional manuscript.** Student self-other distinctive agreement predicts later interaction/liking; positivity also matters. Initial liking association was marginal, and assumed similarity did not significantly predict longer-term development. Self-report agreement is the criterion, not objective whole-person truth. Warmth, flattery and assumed similarity must stay separate from person-specific evidence.

## REL-01

[Laurenceau, Barrett & Pietromonaco (1998), Intimacy as an Interpersonal Process](https://www.affective-science.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/LaurenFBPl1998.pdf)

**Two original event-contingent diary studies; full author-hosted article.** Disclosure/perceived responsiveness relate to intimacy in short undergraduate diary periods; responsiveness partially mediates associations. Relative direct importance reverses between studies and coefficients vary. Correlational single-person perception permits reverse causation/third variables. Helpful intent is not perceived helpfulness; do not import intimacy goals or maximize professional disclosure.

## NEG-01

[Thompson (1991), Information Exchange in Negotiation](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research/detail/1991/information-exchange-in-negotiation)

**Original two-experiment research; institutional abstract only.** Reports information seeking/provision benefits for judgment accuracy and joint outcomes, without individual disadvantage in studied provision conditions. Missing full methods, payoff and sample detail preclude an exact protocol. Lack of disadvantage here does not establish disclosure safety, particularly for reservation values or outside options. Background only.

## NEG-02

[Steinel & De Dreu (2004), Social Motives and Strategic Misrepresentation in Social Decision Making](https://www.ovid.com/journals/jpspy/pdf/00005205-200403000-00004~social-motives-and-strategic-misrepresentation-in-social)

**Original four-experiment research; publisher abstract only.** Information-provision games report selective withholding/inaccuracy under competitive incentives; prosocial orientation did not ensure accuracy. This is incentive-context evidence, not a person-specific lie detector. Omission has multiple explanations; attribute consequential claims and check them against independent evidence.

## NEG-03

[Zarankin & Wall (2012), Negotiators’ Information Sharing](https://ncmr.lps.library.cmu.edu/article/172/galley/175/view/)

**Original randomized laboratory experiment; full journal PDF.** Three-issue job negotiations with 120 undergraduates and trained confederates show sharing/requesting affects disclosure; prior-outcome information did not significantly alter sharing, and aspiration changes did not necessarily increase it. Disclosure frequency includes BATNA/payoffs: it measures neither truth nor advisable/durable benefit. Requests can elicit risky information; volume is not success.

## NEG-04

[Harvard Program on Negotiation: Principled Negotiation—Focus on Interests to Create Value](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/negotiation-skills-daily/principled-negotiation-focus-interests-create-value/)

**Professional framework summary; full practitioner article.** Interests versus positions, options, criteria and alternatives supply useful negotiation vocabulary. This is not primary empirical validation or a guarantee of shared interests. Priorities can be incompatible, a position can be a firm boundary, and a preferable outside option can justify walking away.

## NEG-05

[Harvard Program on Negotiation: How Much Should You Share at the Negotiation Table?](https://www.pon.harvard.edu/daily/business-negotiations/how-much-should-you-share/)

**Professional disclosure guidance; full practitioner article.** Distinguishes facts, predictions/opinions and preferences while considering benefits/costs of disclosure. It is not an empirical algorithm or legal advice; tactical companion advice is not adopted. Rapport does not establish unrestricted trust, and conflict does not prove deception. Disclosure volume is not a criterion of model quality.

## SALE-01

[Franke & Park (2006), Salesperson Adaptive Selling Behavior and Customer Orientation: A Meta-Analysis](https://doi.org/10.1509/jmkr.43.4.693)

**Meta-analysis; publisher abstract/reference list only.** Across 155 salesperson samples, adaptive selling relates to self-, manager- and objective performance; customer orientation shows only the self-rated performance pattern. Observational associations/structural models do not establish causal agent efficacy. Adapt to actual context and distinguish outcome types; customer-centered intent alone proves no sales benefit.

## SALE-02

[Terho, Giovannetti & Cardinali (2022), Measuring B2B social selling](https://u-pad.unimc.it/retrieve/4eb2c769-3a96-42ea-9126-91b9161ac2d2/Terho_2022_SOCIAL%20SELLING_1-s2.0-S0019850121002650.pdf)

**Scale-development/structural-model surveys; full institutional article.** Italian and UK B2B salesperson surveys distinguish insight, connecting and engagement. Acquisition/thought-leadership associations coexist with nonsignificant total retention, direct outcome-performance and retention paths. Single-respondent observational measures and common-method/discriminant concerns limit causal inference. Online activity is not relationship depth; acquisition and retention require separate evaluation.

## SALE-03

[Terho, Haas, Eggert & Ulaga (2012), Conceptualizing Value-Based Selling in Business Markets](https://www.utupub.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/166677/IMM_Terho%20et%20al_Conceptualizing%20Value-Based%20Selling%20in%20Business%20Markets.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y)

**Exploratory qualitative conceptualization; full institutional manuscript.** Experienced managers from 11 international vendor companies inform business-context understanding, value propositions and value-in-use communication. Successful-vendor selection lacks customer-side triangulation or causal outcome testing; authors note limited applicability. Genuine new interest may develop, but each benefit needs evidence and may fail on cost, risk or fit. No-sale remains valid.

## REACT-01

[Dillard & Shen (2005), On the Nature of Reactance and its Role in Persuasive Health Communication](https://doi.org/10.1080/03637750500111815)

**Two original health-message studies; publisher abstract/author notes only.** Anger/negative cognitions relate to perceived freedom threat and attitude/intention in flossing/alcohol messages. Self-report measures and uninspected full methods do not support transcript-only reactance detection or purchase effects. A refusal can reflect substantive disagreement, cost or timing; a stable trait does not follow from a single response.

## MKT-01

[Aguirre et al. (2015), Unraveling the Personalization Paradox](https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/en/publications/unraveling-the-personalization-paradox-the-effect-of-information-/)

**Exploratory field evidence plus three experiments; institutional abstract only.** Collection transparency changes responses to personalized advertising; covert collection can backfire through vulnerability. Exact samples/randomization remain unverified at this depth, and clicks/intentions do not measure relationship understanding. More personal detail is not always better. This source concerns collection context and advertising response, rather than person-model accuracy.

## MKT-02

[Blake, Nosko & Tadelis (2014 working paper;2015 journal), Consumer Heterogeneity and Paid Search Effectiveness](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20171)

**Original paid-search field experiments; NBER author abstract only.** eBay experimental returns were smaller than conventional estimates, with no measurable short-term brand-keyword benefit and heterogeneous non-brand effects. Platform/channel/history limits apply; no blanket dismissal of marketing follows. A purchase after contact does not establish incrementality. This motivates separate causal evaluation, not a relationship-technique efficacy claim.

## EVAL-01

[Camerer et al. (2018), Evaluating the replicability of social science experiments in Nature and Science](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-018-0399-z)

**Preregistered replication project; publisher metadata abstract only.** Of 21 selected Nature/Science experiments, 13 replicated significantly in the same direction; average effects were about half originals. Alternative indicators differ. The journal/year selection is not a universal failure rate or direct test of these methods. Retain selection, criterion and uncertainty with any count.

## EVAL-02

[Holzmeister et al. (online2024;volume2025), Examining replicability of online experiments selected by a decision market](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02062-9)

**Preregistered replication-selection study; publisher metadata abstract only.** Scientists traded on 41 selected PNAS online studies; 26 were replicated, including market extremes. Significance-based replication was 54%, with mean effects 45% of originals; alternative criteria differ. Deliberate selection prevents a representative rate claim. Market confidence is not individual certainty or substitute for qualification.

## What remains unestablished

Independent Clean Language outcome superiority; naturalistic professional/cross-cultural and long-term dyadic transfer; customer-side causal value-selling evidence; agent-mediated benefits/harms; reliable response rubrics; and comparative decision-model/LLM performance remain open. The unrecovered primary perspective-getting paper is not a basis for numerical or experimental claims. No scarcity, priming, social-proof or personality-targeting recipe is licensed here.

Keep offline held-out contract evaluation separate from causal relationship/sales outcomes. A model can follow a rubric accurately without improving a relationship; a sale can occur without incremental agent benefit. This index reports prior source access; it does not imply new retrieval, replication, live testing or any private-data use during skill construction.

The cited studies examine human interactions, ratings or professional practice, not this asynchronous agent workflow. They inform optional human preparation approaches and cautious retrospective interpretation; they establish no live monitoring capability or efficacy of PREPARE/DIGEST. Adaptation across conversations must follow the actual notes/transcript evidence, not an imagined enactment of preparation suggestions.
