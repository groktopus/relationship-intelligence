# Persona semantic quality contract — 1.0.0

Load alongside the Persona output contract when synthesizing, reviewing or accepting a person model. The fixed section format remains unchanged. This contract governs analytical completeness, not personality measurement or a minimum length.

## One person is one analysis

Queue size changes elapsed time, never attention or acceptance quality. Review each person's evidence and draft individually, even when processing a batch. A template is a presentation aid, not the unit of reasoning. Do not compress a queue into one bulk summary operation.

Scale depth to available usable evidence, its richness, relevance, attribution and diversity of context. Conversation count describes coverage; it is neither a hypothesis quota nor an automatic confidence multiplier. Repeated exports, quoted passages and multiple judgments of the same event do not corroborate each other. A rich single conversation can support several contextual distinctions; many repetitive conversations may add little.

## Review the source before reducing it

Recall the existing model and corrections. Identify which original material was actually reviewed, which passages are excerpt-only, what is unavailable and what analysis remains unperformed. Read surrounding turns needed to interpret ownership, response, qualification and conversational influence. Do not infer a person's conviction from another speaker's proposal or from agreement alone.

Use a private working evidence map, or equivalent reasoning notes, for consequential patterns:

- Attributed direct statement, observed interaction or third-party claim; source and passage.
- Context and event-time precision; duplicate/overlap lineage and attribution limits.
- Candidate explanation and what connects the observations beyond a shared topic.
- Situational or competing explanation, counterexample and correction.
- What would distinguish the accounts and why the distinction changes guidance.

This working map need not become a large table in the final note. Preserve enough derivation in the prose that a later agent can inspect and update the explanation. A summary is an index to evidence, not a substitute for reviewing raw context when needed for a deeper inference.

## Evidence-proportional completeness

First identify the consequential distinctions supported or challenged by the source material. Then decide which belong in the model. Do not start by allocating one paragraph to every person or assigning a fixed number of traits.

A rich model should connect motives, priorities, decision criteria, relational expectations and contextual behavior where evidence permits. Explain tensions and exceptions: two apparently opposed preferences may concern different tasks, stakes or periods. Keep distinct hypotheses separable when their evidence, alternatives or practical consequences differ. Do not merge them into a vague label such as pragmatic, collaborative or curious.

Show how understanding develops across conversations. New material may corroborate, narrow, contradict or merely repeat earlier evidence. Distinguish a changed person or priority from new information about an old pattern. Unknown event dates cannot establish a trajectory. Discuss material counterevidence rather than burying it in citations. Claim-level confidence can differ within one model. For each material interpretation, make its supporting evidence, scoped confidence, plausible alternatives and revision conditions individually identifiable. A pooled confidence paragraph or generic statement that context may matter does not satisfy this requirement. These elements may sit beside the interpretation or be cross-referenced concisely; do not duplicate the narrative or impose a hypothesis count. Direct statements and inferred explanations may warrant different confidence within the same interpretation.

Sparse evidence warrants a bounded, useful explanation and explicit gaps. It does not warrant invented motives, padded dimensions or refusal to interpret anything. State whether a gap is **information unavailable**, **attribution unresolved**, or **analysis not yet performed**. Do not report the last category as a limitation of the person or corpus.

## Questions must improve the model

Choose questions from consequential uncertainty in this person's history. Explain what plausible answers would distinguish, what would narrow or overturn the current account, and how subsequent guidance would change. A repeated generic question with the person's name inserted is insufficient. Avoid asking again about something already answered unless a changed context justifies it. Sometimes a practical action, reflection or waiting for relevant evidence is more useful than inquiry.

Examples orient reasoning; generate wording anew. A question does not establish that its premise is true, and preparation does not become observed evidence during digestion.

## Separate acceptance gates

**Source coverage:** Record reviewed scope, material not reviewed, duplicate events, attribution and date limits. If consequential available evidence remains unanalyzed, mark the model partial rather than complete.

**Analytical completeness:** Compare the draft with the source map. Are the important supported distinctions explained, connected and scoped? Did a short organizing story erase consequential exceptions, competing explanations or changes? Can the reviewer identify what evidence would revise each material inference? Does the guidance follow from that understanding?

**Usefulness:** Could this understanding change preparation in a person-specific way? Do the optional questions distinguish real alternatives rather than merely elicit more biography? A model may be limited yet useful; fluent breadth without evidence is not depth.

**Persistence fidelity:** Only after the analytical review passes, reconcile and save through available host capabilities. Read back the actual managed section and verify that its explanations, alternatives, revisions and question branches survived. A writer must not reduce an approved model to facts or chronology. Preserve outside content and user edits.

Record each gate separately as passed, failed, partial or blocked with reasons. Structural validation and exact text retention cannot certify analytical completeness. A successful synthetic evaluation does not prove performance on actual people or causal relationship outcomes. Do not declare all profiles complete because all files have headings or all saves succeeded.
