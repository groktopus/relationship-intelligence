## What this does not establish

No controlled held-out accuracy estimate, calibrated psychological scale, causal relationship benefit or incremental sales effect has been measured. Individual behavior remains scoped to its evidence and context. The research is a targeted cross-field synthesis, not a systematic completeness claim. Real host installation and connector combinations require their own observed qualification.
